Share places directly to VenuZ

Once VenuZ is on your home screen, you can share any place straight to your wishlist from any app.

🗺️
Google Maps
Find a place → tap Share → choose VenuZ from the share sheet
📸
Instagram
See a tagged restaurant or bar → tap ··· → Share → VenuZ
🎵
TikTok
Spot a place in a video → tap Share → More → VenuZ
🧭
Safari
Visit any restaurant or venue page → tap the share icon → VenuZ

Don't see VenuZ in your share sheet? Make sure it's added to your home screen first.

Add to home screen →

On iOS: open VenuZ in Safari → tap the share icon → "Add to Home Screen"
