Last updated: May 2026

Overview

VenuZ ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have over it.

We collect only what we need. We do not sell your data. Ever.

What We Collect

At this stage, the only information we collect is:

  • Your email address, submitted voluntarily through our waitlist form
  • Your city, if you choose to provide it
  • The date and time of your submission

We do not collect your name, phone number, payment information, or any other personal data at this time.

How We Use Your Information

Your email address is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To notify you when VenuZ launches or enters early access
  • To send occasional product updates you signed up for
  • To contact you about your waitlist status

We will never send you spam or sell your email to third parties.

Data Storage

Your information is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted cloud database provider. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We retain your information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Ask us to delete your information at any time
  • Opt out of communications at any time by contacting us directly

To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address below and we will respond within 5 business days.

Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We may use trusted service providers (such as email delivery services) to help us operate VenuZ, but they are bound to keep your information confidential.

Changes to This Policy

As VenuZ grows, this policy may be updated to reflect new features or legal requirements. We will always post the updated date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will notify waitlist members by email.
